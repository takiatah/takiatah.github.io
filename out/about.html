About

Takia Tahmid

I'm a third-year Computer Science student at the University at Buffalo and a Data Science Research Assistant, working across software development, web development, machine learning, and data engineering.

What I work on

In Dr. Zhuang's research group I clean and analyze government macroeconomic datasets and build machine learning models that evaluate international trade risk and anticipate disruptions in global supply chains. I also tutor at UB Math Place, and previously led weekly recitations on proofs and graph theory as a Computer Science teaching assistant.

Outside research, my projects range from exploratory sales analysis in Python and SQL and Kaggle competitions to a dynamic memory allocator and chat-server serialization in C, a text adventure in OCaml, and responsive websites. ORBIT collects that work in one place, with each destination focused on a different kind of problem.
